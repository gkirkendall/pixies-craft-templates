TRIP VIDEO: a ready-made project for EffectCraft
================================================

A 60-second trip video with eleven shots: photo shots with titles, lower thirds and map insets, three map cards, a sign-off and
your logo in the corner. Swap in your own photos and text, add music, and export. No scripts or command line needed.

  Trip Video 16x9.ecproj   wide video (1920x1080)
  Trip Video 9x16.ecproj   tall video for phones (1080x1920); the photos are shown whole over a blurred copy of themselves

Keep this whole folder together: the project finds its pictures next to it.

1. Open "Trip Video 16x9.ecproj" in EffectCraft (File > Open). Press the space bar to play it.

2. Your photos. Drag your photos from Finder or File Explorer into the Project panel. Click a shot layer in the timeline
   ("Shot 1 · Photo_1"), open Window > Essential Graphics, and pick your photo in Media. Do the same for each of the eight photo
   shots. Don't use File > Replace Footage: with camera-numbered photos (IMG_2331.JPG) it can bring in the whole numbered run as
   one image sequence. The photos that come with the project (Photo_1 ... Photo_8) are only placeholders.

3. Your text. Click a title or lower-third layer ("Title (Animated Title)", "Place Name · Day 1 (Lower Third)", "Sign-off") and
   type in Window > Essential Graphics. The title fades in from 0.3 seconds: move the playhead past that to see it. The map
   shots show "Stop 1" ... "Stop 6": type your place names in the same panel. New places on the maps (the picture itself):
   see "Make Maps/README.txt".

4. Your logo. Click "Corner Logo (whole video)" and pick your picture in Logo (a PNG with a transparent background works
   best). It shows in the corner for the whole video; Corner, Size and Margin are in the same panel.

5. Music. See "Music/README.txt".

6. File > Export to make your video.
